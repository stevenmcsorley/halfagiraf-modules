## Grainloom

Granular textures, frozen loops and wavetable voices, using Emilie Gillet's original
open-source Beads DSP.

- Four recording qualities: Digital, Vintage digital, Clean tape and Dirty cassette.
- TIME, PITCH, SIZE and SHAPE with four cable-sensitive attenurandomizers.
- Clocked, gated and continuous grain generation; infinite-size delay and beat slicing.
- Original stereo reverb, feedback processing and twelve released-source wavetable banks.
- Frozen recordings saved with the patch.
- Graphite panel, cyan stereo recording display and explicit gain/CV assignment controls.

Bad Sector, MOD1, Entwine and Constellate are included unchanged.

### Download and use

Choose Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Intel or Apple Silicon. Requires Rack 2.6.6+.
Close Rack and place the `.vcvplugin` in the matching `plugins-*` directory inside
your Rack user folder, then restart.

Start at a low monitoring volume, especially with high feedback.

### Notes

The LCD shows the recording buffer and write head, not individual grain positions.
Quality and mono/stereo changes are deferred while frozen. Restoring a patch
preserves its frozen recording but restarts running grains and reverb tails.
Library availability follows VCV's update review and may lag behind this release.

Native tests/builds passed on all four architectures, with pristine-source
comparisons and sanitizer/stress checks. Physical Beads comparison and Mac desktop
testing were not performed; hardware-identical response is not claimed.
